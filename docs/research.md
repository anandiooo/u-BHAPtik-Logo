# Research — BHAPtik

## Library check
- `search_library.py --industry ecommerce-retail --summary` (20 logos): combination marks 45 %, containment 45 %,
  geometric sans 30 %, multi-colour 30 %. The library has no fashion/textile set, so category knowledge below is my own.
- `--query fold`: folded marks in the library are almost all **isometric ribbons** (Strapi, VS Code, Buildkite,
  Sublime, Magento, Nodal). Flat, single-colour folds are open territory, but avoid ribbon/isometric looks.
- `--query petal|knot|clover`: Yii (three gradient petals), Alfresco (petal pinwheel), OpenAI and Chromatic
  (interlaced knots). No four-leaf diagonal kawung form found. Avoid interlaced knots (OpenAI-like).
- Studied for construction: `adyen.svg` (fully custom squared lowercase, one weight, ownable without a symbol),
  `stripe.svg` (tight custom lowercase carries the brand alone), `strapi-icon.svg` (fold expressed by simple flat planes),
  `fastly.svg` / `hubspot.svg` (one letter substitution as the only accent).

## Category conventions (lifestyle accessories / batik retail)
- Batik retail: ornate script or serif, gold, floral parang/mega mendung panels, wayang or canting illustrations,
  brown-and-gold palettes, "heritage" emblems.
- Minimal lifestyle (Muji, Uniqlo, COS, Arket, Pomelo, Cotton Ink): plain grotesque or geometric sans, all caps or
  all lowercase, no symbol or a red box. Clean but generic.
- Gen Z accessory brands: rounded bubble type, smiley faces, stickers, wavy outlines.

## Clichés, off-limits
needle and thread · hanger · clothing tag with string · crown, laurel, gold · cherry blossom, torii, rising sun,
kanji, wave (Hokusai) · full batik pattern panels · canting or wax pot drawn literally · wayang · flower in a circle ·
stock leaf · isometric ribbon folds · interlaced knot.

## Open territory
A solid, friendly custom wordmark (between Adyen's rigour and a Gen Z wink) whose single textile detail rewards a
second look; a symbol built from one batik unit (not a pattern) or one fold.
