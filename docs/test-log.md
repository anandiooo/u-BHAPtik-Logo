renders via Chrome. Everything is generated by `build.py <version>` into `out/`.

## Loop 1 (v1 → v2)
- Kawung v1: fat round leaves read as a generic flower/butterfly. Tried v2 quarter-arc leaves: these read as an **X**,
  and next to the wordmark it read "X BHAPtik". Rejected.
- Open Fold v1: a diamond quartered by a + gap looked generic (Renault/window icon). v2 unfolds the top-right flap
  across its 45° crease. The silhouette is now ownable and shows the action of unwrapping.
- i-dot v2 (a thin 45° leaf) read as an acute accent: "BHAPtík".

## Loop 2 (v3 → v5)
- Kawung v3: leaves at 0.33 width/length ratio read as four leaves, not an X. i-dot changed to a fat lens (26 × 37)
  that reads as a dot and echoes the symbol.
- Audit v3: k leg at 44.2° (near-miss) → snapped to 45°. Fractional lockup viewBoxes → rounded to integers.
- Open Fold symbol scale reduced (margins 10 → 14) to match the optical size of the other two.
- Pixel test v4: the + gap in Kawung closed at 16 px → v5 moves the leaves out (gap 15 → 21 from centre) and makes
  them slightly shorter. At 16 px the four lobes still separate.
- Audit v5: all six files 100/100, 1 colour, no live text, no strokes; anchors 12 (A), 42 (B), 16 (C).

## Craft pass (v5)
- Letter test: B, H, A, P, t, i, k all read correctly at 32 px and at lockup size. B Link: the P→t bar can read as
  "BHAP-tik" at a glance; this is intended, but it is the risk of that concept.
- Junctions: stems overlap the bars by 1–2 units (no hairline seams). The t foot inner radius of 6 is the smallest
  curve (≈0.6 mm at a 25 mm cap height). Fine for embroidery at ≥ 20 mm wide.
- Mirror/180°: Kawung is symmetric (no new readings). Bt mirrored reads as "†B" (no issue). Open Fold rotated reads as
  an arrow pointing in the opposite direction (no issue).
- Peer test: at 96/32 px all three look as resolved as Framer, Medium and Tidal.
- Known limits: the subpaths overlap and are unioned by the nonzero fill rule. Unite them into single outlines
  (Pathfinder → Unite) before embroidery digitising. No trademark search has been done.
