## Design brief — BHAPtik

**Summary**: An Indonesian lifestyle-accessories brand (totes, caps, keychains, small goods) that reworks batik with
everyday Japanese-style minimalism and furoshiki wrapping. Modern lifestyle brand first, batik second.
**Audience**: Gen Z, urban Indonesia first, then regional. They meet the mark on Instagram/TikTok avatars, woven
neck labels, embroidered caps, tote prints, hang tags, keychain charms and packaging.
**Brand adjectives**: modern · minimal · playful · wearable · rooted   **Avoid feeling**: traditional, ornate, luxury
**Promise / big idea**: Batik you wear every day without thinking "batik".
**Competitive landscape**: Batik retailers use ornate script, gold, flowers and full pattern panels. Minimal
lifestyle brands (Muji, Uniqlo, COS, Arket, Pomelo) use plain grotesque wordmarks with nothing ownable. Open territory:
a friendly, solid, custom wordmark with one quiet textile detail that rewards a second look.
**Mark type hypothesis**: custom wordmark (the name carries the brand) + a compact symbol or monogram for avatars,
embroidery and keychains, which the wordmark can't do at 16 px (7 letters).
**Must work on**: 16 px avatar/favicon, 10 mm woven label, cap embroidery (no hairlines, min gaps ~0.8 mm at 25 mm),
one-colour screen print, reversed cream-on-black, kraft packaging.
**Constraints**: exact spelling and casing "BHAPtik"; palette warm cream, deep black, earthy brown, restrained pink;
must work in pure black/white; flat only: no gradients, shadows, 3D.
**Success criteria**: reads "BHAPtik" instantly; a contemporary fashion brand at first glance; the batik link
discoverable on second look; symbol recognisable at 16 px and embroiderable; distinct from Muji/Uniqlo/COS.
**Decision-maker**: user   **Deliverables (this round)**: three greyscale concepts + colour hint, then checkpoint.
**Assumptions I made**: mixed case kept as a design feature; "tik" echoes batik (ba-tik); no existing equity;
three concepts mix mark types; files in projects/bhaptik/.

## Word map

```
BHAPtik → B·H·A·P caps + "tik" lowercase; ba-TIK; tik = a dot, a tick, a small mark
batik   → wax-resist (lines left undyed), canting dot, kawung (four ovals round a point),
          parang (45° diagonal bands), ceplok (repeat tile), cracks (remukan), dye layers
furoshiki → a square cloth, corners folded to centre, a knot, wrapping, one square many uses
modern / minimal → flat, solid, geometric, square, one clean detail
playful / Gen Z → rounded corners, chunky weight, a wink, stickers, avatars
wearable → labels, patches, fabric, folds, stitches
Intersections (circled):
  • furoshiki square + kawung → a square cloth with four corners folded to the centre: four petals = kawung
  • wax-resist + letterforms → letters with a consistent "resist" cut, like a line left undyed
  • tik (dot) + canting → the i dot as the brand's one batik dot / a kawung dot
  • case switch P|t + fold → the join between caps and lowercase as a fabric fold
  • parang 45° + type → one shared 45° cut on terminals, parang rhythm
```
