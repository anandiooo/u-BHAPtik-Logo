Final design

Furoshiki Bundle

A furoshiki cloth tied into a bundle, printed with parang rusak and wrapping a row of seigaiha waves.

  • Furoshiki = the Japanese way of wrapping, the brand's product world
  • Parang band with its mlinjo bars = the Indonesian batik side, kept bold, not intricate
  • Seigaiha waves = the Japanese pattern side; one connected ink shape for print, embroidery and stamps
  • Small-size cut with one band and one wave row for favicon, avatar and woven label
